AURA - how to install on your own PC (once)

1. Download this folder (GitHub: green Code button > Download ZIP) and unzip it anywhere, for example Documents.
2. Double-click Install-Colleague.bat. It sets everything up for your PC (your own graphics card runs the free helper)
   and puts an "Aura" icon on your desktop. If it asks you to close the window and run it again, do that.
   - You need your own Claude account (the installer opens the sign-in).
3. Open "Aura" from your desktop.

Updates come from Amar: Settings > Update, then close Aura and open it again.
Problems: double-click check_setup.bat and send the result to Amar.
